Gennady Korotkevich (handle: **tourist**) is widely considered the greatest competitive programmer in history. He started winning major contests as a child and built a record that is difficult for anyone else to match. ([Wikipedia][1])

Some of his achievements:

* Six consecutive gold medals at the International Olympiad in Informatics (IOI), including multiple first-place finishes. ([Wikipedia][1])
* ICPC World Champion (team competition) in 2013 and 2015. ([Wikipedia][1])
* Winner of major contests such as Google Code Jam, Facebook Hacker Cup, Yandex.Algorithm, Russian Code Cup, VK Cup, and many others. ([cphof.org][2])
* Held or reached the highest ratings on platforms like Codeforces, TopCoder, AtCoder, CodeChef, and HackerRank. ([Wikipedia][1])
* In 2024, he became the first person to cross a Codeforces rating of 4000, leading Codeforces to temporarily create a special "Tourist" rank. ([Wikipedia][1])

### Do programmers treat him as a god?

Not literally, but within competitive programming he is probably the closest thing to a "GOAT" (Greatest Of All Time).

Think of it this way:

| Field                   | Person often considered GOAT |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Chess                   | Magnus Carlsen               |
| Sprinting               | Usain Bolt                   |
| Competitive Programming | Gennady Korotkevich          |

When top programmers discuss competitive programming, "tourist" is usually the benchmark. People often say things like:

* "Can you solve it as fast as tourist?"
* "Tourist already solved it."
* "Tourist is playing a different game."

His consistency is what impresses people most. Many competitors have one great year. Tourist has remained near the top for well over a decade. ([Codeforces][3])

### But do all programmers worship him?

No.

There are different kinds of programmers:

1. **Competitive programmers**
   Almost all know who tourist is, and many regard him as the GOAT.

2. **Software engineers**
   Many respect him but may care more about system design, distributed systems, product engineering, databases, cloud architecture, etc.

3. **Researchers and AI scientists**
   They may admire his algorithmic skills but judge greatness differently, based on research impact.

A common saying is:

> Competitive programming skill and software engineering skill overlap, but they are not the same thing.

So tourist is an undisputed legend in competitive programming, but software development is a much larger field.

### Why is he so respected?

Because even other world champions struggle to match his record.

For example, reaching a Codeforces rating above 3000 already puts someone among the world's elite. Tourist not only exceeded that, he became the first person to break 4000. ([Codeforces][4])

Among competitive programmers, if you hear someone say:

> "He's the tourist of our university."

that's usually one of the highest compliments possible.
