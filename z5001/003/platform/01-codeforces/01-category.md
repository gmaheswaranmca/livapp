The **4000 mark** refers to **Codeforces rating**, **not the number of problems solved**.

### What is Codeforces?

[Codeforces](https://codeforces.com?utm_source=chatgpt.com) is one of the world's most popular competitive programming platforms. Contest participants solve algorithmic problems, and after each rated contest, their rating changes based on performance.

### How does the rating work?

It's similar to chess Elo ratings:

| Rating    | Rank                      |
| --------- | ------------------------- |
| < 1200    | Newbie                    |
| 1200–1399 | Pupil                     |
| 1400–1599 | Specialist                |
| 1600–1899 | Expert                    |
| 1900–2099 | Candidate Master          |
| 2100–2299 | Master                    |
| 2300–2399 | International Master      |
| 2400–2599 | Grandmaster               |
| 2600–2999 | International Grandmaster |
| 3000+     | Legendary Grandmaster     |

For most competitive programmers:

* 1600 = good
* 1900 = very good
* 2200 = elite
* 2400+ = among the world's best
* 3000+ = extremely rare

### What does 4000 mean?

When Gennady Korotkevich crossed **4000 rating**, it meant that his contest performance was so far ahead of other elite programmers that even the existing rating categories didn't adequately describe it.

To give perspective:

* Many strong university competitive programmers: 1600–2200
* ICPC World Finalists: often 2400–3000+
* Top 10 Codeforces users: roughly 3500–3900+
* Tourist: crossed **4000**

### Is rating based on number of problems solved?

No.

Suppose:

* Person A solves 10,000 easy problems.
* Person B solves 5 difficult problems in a contest and beats top competitors.

Person B can have a much higher rating.

Rating depends mainly on:

1. Contest performance
2. Difficulty of opponents
3. Rank achieved in contests

### Example

Imagine a Codeforces contest with 30,000 participants.

* Rank 20,000 → rating may decrease.
* Rank 5,000 → small increase.
* Rank 500 → large increase.
* Rank 1–10 repeatedly against elite competitors → very high rating.

Tourist has been finishing near the top of major contests for more than a decade, which is why his rating became so extraordinary.
