Anyone can practice ICPC-style problems, but **participating in the official ICPC contest** has eligibility rules.

## Who Can Participate in ICPC?

The contest is intended for university students.

Generally, you must:

* Be enrolled in a degree-granting program at a university or college.
* Meet ICPC age and participation limits for the season.
* Compete as part of a team of **three students** representing their institution.

The exact eligibility rules can vary slightly by season, so contestants should always check the current rules from the official ICPC website.

## Can School Students Participate?

Officially, ICPC is for university students.

However, school students often prepare by participating in:

* International Olympiad in Informatics (IOI)
* National Olympiads in Informatics
* Codeforces contests
* AtCoder contests
* CodeChef contests

Many future ICPC champions start competitive programming in school.

---

## Can Working Professionals Participate?

You cannot participate in the official ICPC if you do not meet the student eligibility requirements.

However, professionals can still solve ICPC archives and participate in online contests on:

* [Codeforces](https://codeforces.com?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
* [AtCoder](https://atcoder.jp?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
* [CodeChef](https://www.codechef.com?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
* [Kattis](https://open.kattis.com?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Many top programmers continue competing long after graduation.

---

## What About Trainers and Teachers?

Teachers, trainers, and coaches cannot usually compete as official ICPC contestants, but they can:

* Coach university teams
* Create training material
* Conduct mock contests
* Solve ICPC problems for practice

Many famous competitive programming coaches were former ICPC contestants.

## If You Are Learning Competitive Programming Today

Since you're interested in data structures and algorithms, you can follow the same path many ICPC competitors take:

1. Arrays & Strings
2. Sorting & Searching
3. Linked Lists
4. Stack & Queue
5. Trees
6. Graphs
7. Greedy Algorithms
8. Dynamic Programming
9. Number Theory
10. Advanced Data Structures

After that, start participating regularly in contests on [Codeforces](https://codeforces.com?utm_source=chatgpt.com). Reaching around **Specialist (1400+)**, then **Expert (1600+)**, then **Candidate Master (1900+)** is a strong progression toward solving many ICPC regional-level problems.
