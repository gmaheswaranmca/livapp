### Tip to Remember

* **Kannada:** Ondu, Eradu, Mooru, Naalku, Aidu...
* **Telugu:** Okaṭi, Rendu, Moodu, Naalugu, Aidu...
* **Hindi:** Ek, Do, Teen, Chaar, Paanch...
* **Malayalam:** Onnu, Randu, Moonnu, Naalu, Anchu...

This covers the first **20 numbers** in all five languages with easy English and Tamil pronunciations.
