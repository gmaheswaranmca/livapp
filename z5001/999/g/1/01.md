### Types of Pronouns

1. Personal Pronouns (I, You, He, She, We, They)
2. Object Pronouns (Me, Him, Her, Us, Them)
3. Possessive Adjectives (My, Your, His, Her, Our, Their)
4. Possessive Pronouns (Mine, Yours, Ours, Theirs)
5. Reflexive Pronouns (Myself, Yourself, Himself, Herself, Ourselves, Themselves)

This provides the **most commonly used beginner pronouns**. The next recommended category is **Articles (a, an, the)** followed by **Helping Verbs (am, is, are, was, were, have, has, had, do, does, did, will, shall, can, could, may, might, must, should, etc.)**.
