## English Vocabulary – Articles

| English | Kannada (English) | Kannada (Tamil) | Telugu (English) | Telugu (Tamil) | Hindi (English)     | Hindi (Tamil) | Malayalam (English) | Malayalam (Tamil) |
| ------- | ----------------- | --------------- | ---------------- | -------------- | ------------------- | ------------- | ------------------- | ----------------- |
| A       | Ondu              | ஒந்து           | Oka              | ஒக             | Ek                  | ஏக்           | Oru                 | ஒரு               |
| An      | Ondu              | ஒந்து           | Oka              | ஒக             | Ek                  | ஏக்           | Oru                 | ஒரு               |
| The     | Aa / A            | ஆ / அ           | Aa               | ஆ              | Vah / Yah (context) | வஹ் / யஹ்     | Aa                  | ஆ                 |

### Usage

| Article | When to Use                                                 | Example                                |
| ------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| **A**   | Before a singular noun beginning with a **consonant sound** | A boy, A cat, A university             |
| **An**  | Before a singular noun beginning with a **vowel sound**     | An apple, An egg, An umbrella, An hour |
| **The** | For a **specific** person, place, or thing already known    | The sun, The book, The teacher         |

### Important Note

Unlike English, **Kannada, Telugu, Hindi, and Malayalam do not have direct equivalents of the English articles** **a**, **an**, and **the**. Their meanings are usually conveyed by context:

* **A / An** ≈ **one** (Ondu / Oka / Ek / Oru) when emphasis is needed.
* **The** usually has **no separate word**. It indicates a specific noun through context.

### Examples

| English  | Kannada     | Telugu     | Hindi     | Malayalam    |
| -------- | ----------- | ---------- | --------- | ------------ |
| A boy    | Ondu huduga | Oka abbayi | Ek ladka  | Oru aankutty |
| An apple | Ondu sebu   | Oka seeb   | Ek seb    | Oru apple    |
| The boy  | Aa huduga   | Aa abbayi  | Vah ladka | Aa aankutty  |
