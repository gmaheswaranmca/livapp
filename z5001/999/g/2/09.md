* City → **Nagara / Nagaram / Shahar / Nagaram**

### Travel

* Railway Station → **Railu Nildana / Railway Station / Railway Station / Railway Station**

### Public Places

* School → **Shaale / Paatashaala / School / School**
* Hospital → **Aaspatre / Aasupatri / Aspataal / Aashupathri**
* Bank → **Bank** (common in all languages)
* Market → **Santhe / Santa / Bazaar / Chantha**

### Religious Places

* Temple → **Devasthana / Devalayam / Mandir / Kshethram**
* Church → **Church / Church / Girja / Palli**
* Mosque → **Masidi / Masidu / Masjid / Palli**

# Important Notes

* Many modern place names such as **Bank, ATM, College, Hotel, Mall, Cafe, Clinic, Stadium,** and **Restaurant** are commonly borrowed from English in Kannada, Telugu, and Malayalam.
* Malayalam **Palli** can refer to both a **church** and a **mosque**, depending on the context.
* Hindi **Bazaar** is the most common word for **market**.
* Telugu **Ooru** means **village** or **native place** and is widely used in everyday conversation.
