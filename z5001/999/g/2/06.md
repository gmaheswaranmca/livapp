# Quick Memory Tips

### Basic Colors

| English | Kannada    | Telugu         | Hindi     | Malayalam    |
| ------- | ---------- | -------------- | --------- | ------------ |
| Red     | **Kempu**  | **Erupu**      | **Laal**  | **Chuvappu** |
| Blue    | **Neeli**  | **Neelam**     | **Neela** | **Neela**    |
| Green   | **Hasiru** | **Aakupaccha** | **Hara**  | **Pacha**    |
| Yellow  | **Haladi** | **Pasupu**     | **Peela** | **Manja**    |
| Black   | **Kappu**  | **Nalupu**     | **Kaala** | **Karuppu**  |
| White   | **Bili**   | **Telupu**     | **Safed** | **Vella**    |

### Precious Colors

* Silver → **Belli / Vendi / Chandi / Velli**
* Gold → **Bangara / Bangaram / Sona / Swarnam**

### Easy to Remember

* **Gulabi** = Pink (Kannada, Telugu, Hindi)
* **Laal** = Red (Hindi)
* **Safed** = White (Hindi)
* **Hara** = Green (Hindi)
* **Peela** = Yellow (Hindi)

---

# Important Notes

* **Pink** is commonly called **Gulabi** in Kannada, Telugu, and Hindi.
* Malayalam **Karuppu** (black) and Tamil **கருப்பு** are very similar.
* Kannada **Kempu** (red) is one of the most commonly used color words.
* Telugu **Pasupu** means **yellow**, and it is also the word for **turmeric**, because turmeric is naturally yellow.
* Many modern shades such as **Cream, Beige, Cyan, Magenta, Turquoise, Navy Blue** are commonly referred to by their English names in everyday speech.
