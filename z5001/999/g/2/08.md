# Important Notes

* Kannada **Huli** and Telugu **Puli** both mean **tiger**, while Malayalam commonly uses **Kaduva** or **Puli**.
* Hindi **Billi** (cat) and Telugu **Pilli** (cat) sound very similar, making them easy to remember.
* Malayalam **Patti** means **dog**, whereas in Tamil **பட்டி** usually means **hamlet/village**, so don't confuse the meanings.
* **Zebra, Panda, Koala, Giraffe, Alligator,** and **Kangaroo** are commonly referred to by their English names in everyday speech.
