📚 *Beginner in Data Structures?* 

Focus on these key topics:

- *Arrays* – Basics, operations, fixed size  
- *Linked Lists* – Singly, Doubly, Circular  
- *Stacks* – LIFO, push/pop, real-life examples  
- *Queues* – FIFO, types (circular, priority)  
- *Hash Tables* – Key-value pairs, collisions  
- *Trees* – Binary Trees, BSTs, traversals (Inorder, Preorder, Postorder)  
- *Graphs* – Nodes & edges, DFS, BFS basics  
- *Heaps* – Min-heap, Max-heap, applications  
- *Recursion* – Base & recursive cases, stack behind recursion  
- *Big-O Notation* – Time & space complexity analysis

✅ Master logic + practice problems = real progress
