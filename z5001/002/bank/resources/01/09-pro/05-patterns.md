🔥 *Top 10 Common DSA Patterns You Must Master* 🧩

Recognizing patterns helps you solve problems faster in interviews. Here are the key ones:

1️⃣ *Sliding Window*  
– Used in arrays/strings for subarray problems.  
*e.g.,* Max sum subarray of size k

2️⃣ *Two Pointers*  
– Great for sorted arrays, finding pairs.  
*e.g.,* Check if array has 2 numbers that sum to target

3️⃣ *Fast & Slow Pointers (Tortoise Method)*  
– Detect cycles in linked lists.  
*e.g.,* Linked list cycle, middle of linked list

4️⃣ *Binary Search*  
– Use on sorted arrays or to optimize answers.  
*e.g.,* Find target in sorted array, minimum in rotated array

5️⃣ *DFS & BFS*  
– Tree/graph traversal.  
*e.g.,* Level order traversal, island counting

6️⃣ *Backtracking*  
– Try all combinations and backtrack.  
*e.g.,* N-Queens, Sudoku solver, subsets

7️⃣ *Dynamic Programming (DP)*  
– Break into subproblems, store results.  
*e.g.,* Fibonacci, knapsack, longest common subsequence

8️⃣ *Greedy*  
– Make locally optimal choices.  
*e.g.,* Activity selection, coin change (sometimes)

9️⃣ *Heap / Priority Queue*  
– Always access min/max element efficiently.  
*e.g.,* K largest elements, median in stream

🔟 *Union-Find (Disjoint Set)*  
– Used in graphs for cycle detection & connected components.
*e.g.,* Kruskal’s algorithm, number of islands (optimized)

💡 *Pattern > Memorization.* Once you master these, you’ll crack most DSA problems with ease.
