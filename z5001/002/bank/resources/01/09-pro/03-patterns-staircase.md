✅ *Master DSA with This 15-Step Pattern Staircase* 🧠🚀  

*🔹 BASICS (Steps 1–5):*  
1️⃣ *Sliding Window* – Efficiently handle subarrays for min/max/sum problems  
2️⃣ *Two Pointers* – Use two indices to solve pair problems (e.g. reverse, partition)  
3️⃣ *Prefix Sum* – Precompute sums for fast range calculations  
4️⃣ *Tree Traversal* – InOrder, PreOrder, PostOrder for tree-based logic  
5️⃣ *Matrix Traversal* – Navigate grids in all patterns (spiral, zigzag, etc.)

*🔸 INTERMEDIATE (Steps 6–10):*  
6️⃣ *DFS (Depth First Search)* – Solve maze, path, and graph search problems  
7️⃣ *BFS (Breadth First Search)* – Find shortest paths, layer-by-layer traversal  
8️⃣ *Backtracking* – Solve permutations, combinations, and puzzles  
9️⃣ *Dynamic Programming* – Optimize subproblems using memoization & tabulation  
🔟 *Fast & Slow Pointers* – Detect cycles, middle nodes, or duplicates in linked lists

*🔺 ADVANCED (Steps 11–15):*  
1️⃣1️⃣ *In-Place Reversal* – Reverse arrays/linked lists without extra space  
1️⃣2️⃣ *Monotonic Stack* – Use increasing/decreasing stacks for histogram or span problems  
1️⃣3️⃣ *Binary Search* – Efficient search in sorted arrays and boundaries
1️⃣4️⃣ *Top K Elements* – Use heaps to find k-largest/smallest (leaderboards, frequency)  
1️⃣5️⃣ *Overlapping Intervals* – Handle merging, intersection, and scheduling

📌 *Tip:* Master these patterns, and you’ll be interview-ready for any DSA round!
