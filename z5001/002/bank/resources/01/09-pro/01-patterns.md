✅ *10 Leetcode DSA Patterns You Should Master (With Real Examples)* 📚💻  

1️⃣ *Two Pointers*  
– Used in: *Container With Most Water, Remove Duplicates from Sorted Array*  
– Pattern: Start from both ends, move based on condition.

2️⃣ *Sliding Window*  
– Used in: *Longest Substring Without Repeating Characters*  
– Pattern: Expand/shrink window to track subarrays/substrings.

3️⃣ *Binary Search*  
– Used in: *Search in Rotated Sorted Array, Find Peak Element*  
– Pattern: Midpoint check to divide and conquer.

4️⃣ *Fast & Slow Pointers*  
– Used in: *Linked List Cycle, Find Duplicate Number*  
– Pattern: Detect cycles or intersections.

5️⃣ *Breadth-First Search (BFS)*  
– Used in: *Binary Tree Level Order Traversal, Word Ladder*  
– Pattern: Level-wise traversal, use a queue.

6️⃣ *Depth-First Search (DFS)*  
– Used in: *Number of Islands, Path Sum*  
– Pattern: Recursive or stack-based deep exploration.

7️⃣ *Backtracking*  
– Used in: *Permutations, N-Queens, Sudoku Solver*  
– Pattern: Try, backtrack if invalid.

8️⃣ *Dynamic Programming*  
– Used in: *Climbing Stairs, Coin Change*  
– Pattern: Store subproblem results to avoid recomputation.

9️⃣ *Greedy*  
– Used in: *Jump Game, Activity Selection*  
– Pattern: Always make the locally optimal choice.

🔟 *Heap / Priority Queue*  
– Used in: *Merge k Sorted Lists, Top K Frequent Elements*  
– Pattern: Efficient min/max tracking.
