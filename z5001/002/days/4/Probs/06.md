# Problem 6: Insertion Sort

## Question Title

Sort an Array Using Insertion Sort

## Category

Sorting

## Difficulty Level

Easy

## Time Complexity

* Best Case: O(n)
* Average Case: O(n²)
* Worst Case: O(n²)

## Space Complexity

* O(1)

---

## Problem Description

Given an array of integers, sort the array in ascending order using the Insertion Sort algorithm.

Insertion Sort builds the sorted array one element at a time by taking each element and inserting it into its correct position among the already sorted elements.

Print the sorted array.

---

## Input Format

* First line contains an integer N representing the number of elements.
* Second line contains N space-separated integers.

---

## Output Format

Print the sorted array in ascending order separated by spaces.

---

## Constraints

* 1 ≤ N ≤ 10³
* -10⁹ ≤ Array[i] ≤ 10⁹

---

## Sample Input

```text
5
12 11 13 5 6
```

## Sample Output

```text
5 6 11 12 13
```

---

## Explanation

Initial Array:

```text
12 11 13 5 6
```

After inserting elements one by one into their correct positions:

```text
11 12 13 5 6
11 12 13 5 6
5 11 12 13 6
5 6 11 12 13
```

Final sorted array:

```text
5 6 11 12 13
```

## Notes

### Insertion Sort Process

```text
Initial:
12 11 13 5 6

Pass 1:
11 12 13 5 6

Pass 2:
11 12 13 5 6

Pass 3:
5 11 12 13 6

Pass 4:
5 6 11 12 13
```

### Characteristics

* Stable Sort
* In-place Sort
* Efficient for small datasets
* Performs well on nearly sorted arrays
* Frequently used as a subroutine inside advanced sorting algorithms

### Key Idea

```text
Take Next Element
↓
Compare with Sorted Portion
↓
Shift Larger Elements Right
↓
Insert at Correct Position
```

### Best Use Case

```text
Nearly Sorted Arrays
Small Input Sizes
Online Sorting
```
