### How to use this list effectively

1. **Don't look at the tags:** When you open a problem, avoid looking at the "Tags" section on Codeforces. Try to identify the pattern yourself for at least 20–30 minutes.
2. **Understand the "Why":** After solving (or failing to solve), read the editorial. Focus on *why* a specific approach was used (e.g., "Why did I need to sort this array?" or "Why does binary search work here?").
3. **Implement from scratch:** Never just copy code. Read the logic and write your own implementation from scratch to ensure you understand the details.
4. **The 20-minute rule:** If you are truly stuck after 20 minutes of active thinking, read the first hint or a small portion of the editorial to nudge your thought process in the right direction, then try again.
5. **Utilize Tools:** If you want to visualize how these patterns look in action,  can help you map out the decision-making process for choosing between Greedy, DP, or Graph approaches.
