# 🧠 Teaching Strategy (This is what makes YOU stand out)

### 1. Use Real-Life Analogies

* EC2 = Renting a computer
* S3 = Google Drive
* IAM = Login + Permissions

---

### 2. Give Daily Tasks

Example:

* Day 2: “Host your own website”
* Day 3: “Secure your server”

---

### 3. Avoid Overloading Services

For Cloud Practitioner, focus only on:

* EC2
* S3
* IAM
* VPC (basic)
* RDS
* CloudWatch
* Pricing

---

# 📦 Tools You Should Use

* AWS Free Tier
* Simple Linux commands
* Browser only (avoid complexity)

---

# ⚠️ Common Mistakes (Avoid These)

❌ Teaching 50+ AWS services
❌ No hands-on
❌ Skipping billing alerts
❌ Ignoring exam pattern
